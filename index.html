Our Google Ads assistant

AiGAMA is used by our team to plan, build, optimize and report on the Google Ads accounts we manage. It is not available to the public.

Plan

Forecasts clicks, conversions and cost for a website and budget, and suggests the right budget.

Team only

Create

Builds a full Search or Performance Max campaign from a website and budget, for approval.

Team only

Optimize

A daily check finds wasted spend and problems, and suggests fixes for approval.

Team only

Reports

Conversions, cost per conversion, wasted spend and what every change did.

Team only

How a change is made

AiGAMA works through the official Google Ads API. A person always makes the final call.

1

AiGAMA suggests

It reads the account’s settings and performance and prepares a change.

2

A specialist approves

Our Google Ads specialist checks the change and approves it with one click.

3

Only then it’s applied

Nothing changes without approval. New campaigns are created paused.

Data & privacy

Used only by our team, on accounts we are authorized to manage.

Google Ads data is used only to manage those accounts. Never sold.

Follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Runs on our own computers. Account data is not kept on a public server.

Questions

No. AiGAMA is an internal tool for our team only. There is no public sign-up or login.

Can you manage my Google Ads?

Write to info@aigama.app and tell us about your business.

Is AiGAMA made by Google?

No. It uses the official Google Ads API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
