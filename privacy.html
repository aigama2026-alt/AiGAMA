2. What AiGAMA is

AiGAMA is an internal tool for planning, creating, optimizing and reporting on Google Ads campaigns. It is used only by our own Google Ads specialists and runs on our own computers. It is not offered to the public, and there is no public sign-up or login.

We use it on our own Google Ads accounts and on client accounts that we manage with the account owner’s permission, usually through a Google Ads manager account. When we manage a client’s account, we process its data on the client’s behalf, under our agreement with the client.

4. Google user data

AiGAMA uses Google APIs: the Google Ads API, the Google Tag Manager API and Sign in with Google. It accesses only the Google Ads accounts and Tag Manager containers we are authorized to manage, and only to provide the features of the tool.

AiGAMA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except to service providers needed to run AiGAMA, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to those affected.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general AI or machine learning models.
  • Our specialists see Google user data only to do the account work they were asked to do, for security, or to comply with law.

Changes to a Google Ads account are made only after a specialist approves them. New campaigns are created paused. The marketing cookies on this website (section 8) are separate and never use data from the tool.

5. AI processing and automated decisions

AiGAMA uses Google’s Gemini API to read websites and account data and to suggest ads and changes. Only the data needed for a task is sent. Under Google’s terms for the paid Gemini API, Google does not use this data to improve its products.

AiGAMA makes no decisions about people with legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). All changes are suggestions until a person approves them.

6. Where data is stored and who we share it with

The AiGAMA tool runs on our own computers. Account data, settings, approvals and change history are stored there, not on a public server. We never sell personal data. We share it only with these providers:

  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (Ireland/USA): Google Ads API, Google Tag Manager API, Sign in with Google and Gemini API for the tool. On this website: Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics (if you allow Analytics) and Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing (if you allow Marketing). For website analytics and ads, Google acts partly as an independent controller under its own policies.
  • GitHub (Microsoft) (USA): hosting of this website.
  • Our email provider: our emails, including the tool’s daily summary emails to our team.

We will update this list before we use a new kind of provider. We may also share data if the law requires it.

8. Cookies on this website

This website uses necessary storage and, only if you allow them, analytics cookies (Google Analytics) and marketing cookies (Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing), loaded through Google Tag Manager. Nothing is sent to Google before you choose, and Google Consent Mode passes on your choice. Fonts are hosted on this site. Details: Cookie Policy. Change your choice: Cookie settings.

9. Security

Our computers are password-protected and access is limited to our team. Google access is given through Google’s own sign-in and can be removed at any time. This website uses HTTPS. If a data breach is likely to put people’s rights at risk, we will tell the authorities and those affected as required by law.
