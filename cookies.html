1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (like your browser’s local storage) are small pieces of data saved on your device by a website. Some are needed for the site to work. Others, like analytics, are optional and need your consent.

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC (USA). It shows us, in summary form, which pages are visited, where visitors come from and what device they use. Google’s advertising features are switched off, and Google receives no consent for ads. We do not use advertising, retargeting or social media cookies. Fonts and images are hosted on our own site, so no data is sent to Google Fonts or other font services.

3. When you use the early access form

When you send the form, your details go to Formspree, which processes them for us. Formspree may use strictly necessary cookies to prevent spam. See our Privacy Policy.

4. Your choice

  • When you first visit, you can choose Accept or Reject. Both are equally easy, and the site works the same either way. Nothing is sent to Google until you click Accept.
  • You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. If you reject after accepting, we delete the Google Analytics cookies.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as “Reject”.
  • You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
