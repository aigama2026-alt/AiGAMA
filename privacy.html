Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: we use your data only to run AiGAMA for you. We never sell it. Nothing changes in your Google Ads account without your approval, and you can disconnect and delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

AiGAMA (“AiGAMA”, “we”, “us”) is an AI-powered tool that helps businesses create, optimize and report on Google Ads campaigns. AiGAMA is operated by [Company legal name], [Address], [Country]. Contact: info@aigama.app.

2. What data we collect

  • Early access sign-up: your email address, website and the product you are interested in.
  • Account data: when you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Google Ads data: when you connect Google Ads, we access the accounts you authorize, including campaigns, ads, keywords, budgets, search terms, performance metrics and account settings.
  • Website data: public information from the website you enter, such as services, address and opening hours, used to build your campaigns.
  • Content you provide: notes, images and logos you upload, and messages you send in the reports chat.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and error reports, used to keep the service secure and working.

3. How we use your data

  • To plan, create, optimize and report on your Google Ads campaigns.
  • To show you suggested changes and apply them only after you approve them.
  • To send you service emails, such as your daily summary and changes waiting for approval.
  • To contact you about early access, if you signed up.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve AiGAMA.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles for any other purpose.

4. Google user data

AiGAMA uses Google APIs, including the Google Ads API, to access the Google Ads accounts you choose to connect. We use this data only to provide and improve the features you see in AiGAMA.

AiGAMA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data or transfer it to third parties, except as needed to provide the service, to comply with law, or with your consent.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not use Google user data to train general AI models.
  • People at AiGAMA do not read your Google user data, unless you ask us for support, it is needed for security or legal reasons, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

Changes to your Google Ads account are made only after you approve them in AiGAMA. New campaigns are created paused.

5. AI processing

To write ads and suggestions, AiGAMA sends the needed parts of your website and account data to AI service providers ([AI provider name]). They process this data on our behalf only to provide the service, under contracts that do not allow them to use it to train their models.

6. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run AiGAMA, such as hosting, email delivery, form handling and AI processing. Each one processes data only on our instructions. We may also share data if required by law.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you disconnect Google Ads or delete your account, we delete your Google Ads data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer. Early access sign-ups are kept until AiGAMA launches for you or until you ask us to remove them.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, limited access and secure storage of access tokens.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can disconnect AiGAMA from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data by emailing info@aigama.app.
  • If you are in the EU or UK, you also have rights under the GDPR, including the right to object and to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The AiGAMA app uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

11. Children

AiGAMA is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 16.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know by email.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: info@aigama.app
