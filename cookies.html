1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (like your browser’s local storage) are small pieces of data saved on your device by a website. Some are needed for the site to work. Others, like analytics, are optional and need your consent.

3. When you use the early access form

When you send the form, your details go to Formspree, which processes them for us. Formspree may use strictly necessary cookies to prevent spam. See our Privacy Policy.

4. Your choice

  • When you first visit, you can choose Accept all, Reject all, or pick Analytics and Marketing separately in Settings. Accepting and rejecting are equally easy, and the site works the same either way. Nothing is sent to Google until you allow it.
  • You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. If you take back your consent, we delete the Google cookies we can reach on this site and stop the Google tools.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as “Reject all”.
  • You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
