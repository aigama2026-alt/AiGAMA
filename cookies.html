1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, are small pieces of data a website saves on your device. Some are needed for the site to work. Others are optional and need your consent.

3. Your choice

  • On your first visit, choose Accept all, Reject all, or pick Analytics and Marketing in Settings. The site works the same either way. Nothing is sent to Google until you allow it.
  • Change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. If you take back consent, we delete the Google cookies we can reach on this site.
  • A Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser counts as “Reject all”.
  • You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
