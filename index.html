Our internal Google Ads assistant.

AiGAMA is the tool we use to plan, build, optimize and report on the Google Ads accounts we manage. It combines AI with Google Ads expert know-how.

How it works Privacy Policy

What it does

Four simple parts, one place for our Google Ads work.

Plan

Forecasts clicks, conversions and cost for a website and budget, and suggests the right budget.

Create

Builds a full Search or Performance Max campaign from a website and budget.

Optimize

A daily check finds wasted spend and problems, and suggests fixes.

Reports

Clear charts: conversions, best campaigns, wasted spend, and what each change did.

How it works

Every change is checked and approved.

AiGAMA connects to Google Ads through the official Google Ads API. It does the expert work. A person always makes the final call.

  1. 1

    Connect

    We sign in with Google and connect only the Google Ads accounts we are authorized to manage.

  2. 2

    Analyze

    AiGAMA reads campaign settings and performance data and prepares suggestions.

  3. 3

    Approve

    A Google Ads specialist reviews each suggestion and approves it with one click.

  4. 4

    Apply

    Only approved changes are made. New campaigns are created paused.

Who uses it

Our team only

AiGAMA is used internally by our Google Ads specialists. There is no public sign-up.

Accounts we manage

We use it only on our own Google Ads accounts and on client accounts we manage with the owner’s permission.

Clients don’t log in

Clients get results and reports from us. They don’t need an AiGAMA account.

Data and security

We treat Google Ads data with care.

Google Ads data is used only to manage those accounts. Never sold, never used for other advertising.

Follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use.

Access tokens are stored encrypted. Access can be removed at any time in the Google account.

Data is deleted within 30 days after an account is disconnected.

Questions

Can I sign up for AiGAMA?

No. AiGAMA is an internal tool and is not offered to the public.

Can you manage my Google Ads?

Write to us at info@aigama.app and tell us about your business.

Does AiGAMA change ads on its own?

No. A specialist approves every change, and new campaigns are created paused.

Is AiGAMA made by Google?

No. AiGAMA uses the official Google Ads API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
