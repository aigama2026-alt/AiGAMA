3. Google user data

AiGAMA uses Google APIs, including the Google Ads API, to access only the Google Ads accounts you choose to connect, and only to provide and improve the features you see in AiGAMA.

AiGAMA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except to service providers that help us run AiGAMA, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale of the service with notice to you.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general AI or machine learning models.
  • No person at AiGAMA reads your Google user data unless you agree to it (for example, when you ask for support), it is needed for security or to comply with law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Changes to your Google Ads account are made only after you approve them. New campaigns are created paused.

4. AI processing and automated decisions

AiGAMA uses AI to analyze your website and account data and to suggest ads and changes. Only the data needed for a task is sent to our AI provider, which processes it on our behalf and is not allowed to use it to train its models.

AiGAMA does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). All changes to your ads are suggestions until you approve them.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, limited access on a need-to-know basis, and secure storage of Google access tokens. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell the authorities and you as required by law.

9. Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access – get a copy of your data.
  • Correct – fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete – ask us to erase your data.
  • Restrict – ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability – get your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Object – object to use based on legitimate interest. You can always object to marketing emails.
  • Withdraw consent – at any time, for example by clicking “unsubscribe” or emailing us. This does not affect use before you withdrew.
  • Complain – to a data protection authority. Our lead authority is the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija). You can also complain in the EU/EEA country where you live or work, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

To use your rights, email info@aigama.app. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity. It’s free, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

You can also remove AiGAMA’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

10. Your rights (United States)

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose (the categories are in section 2; the sources are you, your Google account and your website; the recipients are in section 5).
  • Get a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct and delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.
  • Appeal if we decline your request, by replying to our answer. If we still decline, you can contact your state Attorney General.

We do not use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out. To make a request, email info@aigama.app. You can use an authorized agent with your written permission.

11. Children

AiGAMA is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will tell you by email or in the app before they apply.
