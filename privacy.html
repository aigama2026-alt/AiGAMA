2. What AiGAMA is

AiGAMA is an internal tool for planning, creating, optimizing and reporting on Google Ads campaigns. It is used only by our own Google Ads specialists. It is not offered to the public, and there is no public sign-up or login. We use it on our own Google Ads accounts and on client accounts that we manage with the account owner’s permission.

When we manage a client’s Google Ads account, we process that account’s data on the client’s behalf, under our agreement with the client. The client remains responsible for its own customers’ data.

4. Google user data

AiGAMA uses Google APIs, including the Google Ads API and Sign in with Google, to access only the Google Ads accounts we are authorized to manage, and only to provide the features of the tool.

AiGAMA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to others, except to service providers that help us run AiGAMA, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale with notice to those affected.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general AI or machine learning models.
  • Our specialists see Google Ads data only to do the account work they were asked to do, for security, or to comply with law.

Changes to a Google Ads account are made only after a specialist approves them. New campaigns are created paused. The marketing cookies on this website (section 8) are separate and never use Google user data from the tool.

5. AI processing and automated decisions

AiGAMA uses AI to analyze website and account data and to suggest ads and changes. Only the data needed for a task is sent to our AI provider, which processes it on our behalf and is not allowed to use it to train its models.

AiGAMA does not make decisions about people that have legal or similarly significant effects (Art. 22 GDPR). All changes are suggestions until a person approves them.

We never sell personal data for money. We share it mainly with service providers (processors) that help us run AiGAMA and may use it only on our instructions under a data processing agreement:

  • GitHub (Microsoft), USA – website hosting. Sees website visit data.

For Google Ads and Analytics, Google acts partly as our processor and partly as an independent controller under its own policies.

  • Cloud hosting and AI providers – run the AiGAMA tool and its AI features.

We will update this list before we start using a new kind of provider. You can ask us for the current list at any time. We may also share data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

8. Cookies

This website uses necessary storage, plus, only if you allow them, analytics cookies (Google Analytics) and marketing cookies (Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing), loaded through Google Tag Manager. Nothing is sent to Google before you choose. We use Google Consent Mode so Google’s tags follow your choice. Fonts are hosted on our own site. Details are in our Cookie Policy, and you can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings.

9. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, access only for our team on a need-to-know basis, and encrypted storage of Google access tokens. If a data breach is likely to put people’s rights at risk, we will tell the authorities and those affected as required by law.

10. Your rights (EU, EEA and UK)

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access – get a copy of your data.
  • Correct – fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete – ask us to erase your data.
  • Restrict – ask us to limit how we use your data.
  • Portability – get your data in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Object – object to use based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent – at any time, for example with Cookie settings. This does not affect use before you withdrew.
  • Complain – to a data protection authority. Our lead authority is the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija). You can also complain in the EU/EEA country where you live or work, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

To use your rights, email info@aigama.app. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity. It’s free, unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. If your data is in a client’s Google Ads account, we will pass your request to that client and help them answer it.

Anyone who connected a Google account to AiGAMA can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

11. Your rights (United States)

If you live in a US state with a privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Utah), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose (the categories are in section 3; the recipients are in section 6).
  • Get a copy of, correct and delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. If you allow Marketing cookies, Google Ads remarketing may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some state laws. To opt out, open Cookie settings and turn off Marketing, or use a browser with Global Privacy Control.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.
  • Appeal if we decline your request, by replying to our answer. If we still decline, you can contact your state Attorney General.

We do not use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a request to opt out. To make a request, email info@aigama.app.
